Refunds

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Pro is a prepaid license, not a subscription. If you have not redeemed a key, or you request a refund within 14 days of payment and the key has not been used for more than 48 hours, the seller will refund via the original payment method and revoke the key id.

After that window, refunds are at the seller’s discretion (unused term, duplicate charge, or inability to redeem because checkout was misconfigured).

Revoked keys stop unlocking Pro after the next app build that includes the id in the denylist, or immediately if you contact support and the seller confirms.

Chargebacks without contacting support first may result in the key being revoked.
