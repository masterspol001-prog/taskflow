Help

Where is my data?

In this browser’s localStorage for this site. Export JSON from Settings. There is no cloud sync.

How do I activate Pro?

Pay on the checkout link. After the seller confirms payment, you receive a TF1.… key. Paste it in Settings or the paywall. The app verifies the signature on this device.

I paid and have no key

Fulfillment is manual until the seller wires a webhook. Email the address on your payment receipt with the payment id. The seller re-issues from the sales ledger.

I switched browsers

Pro is per device. Paste the same key again, or ask the seller to re-issue if you lost it. Export JSON before you leave a browser.
